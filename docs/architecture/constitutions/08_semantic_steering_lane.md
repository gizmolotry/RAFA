A time-varying tension envelope can reshape the chamber's harmonic vs inharmonic routing without collapsing the audio substrate.

## What This Lane Exists to Prove

- the chamber can bifurcate and recover in sync with a commanded tension curve

## Core Allowed Modules

- q-band / alpha / temperature / delta steering
- trajectory evaluator over harmonic vs inharmonic energy

## Central Evaluator
- `tension_to_inharmonic_corr`
- `peak_alignment_error`
- `recovery_gap`
