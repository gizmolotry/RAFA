# Constitution: Positive Replacement / Circleworld

## Identity
This branch is the organism, not the lobotomy. It does not carve pieces out of the hybrid. It tries to build a different internal ontology directly.

## Thesis
Meaning should live inside recursive structural mass laws. Coherent structures should be promoted into local subsystems that carry their own law.

## What This Branch Exists to Prove
- the IFS can become a world, not just a solver
- major/minor-arc structure can define promotability
- promoted packets can become active local law
- recursion depth can elaborate local worlds without collapsing into junk

## Core Allowed Modules
- recursive phase field
- Hardy-Littlewood style arc field
- promotability scoring
- passive and active promoted packets
- recursive depth
- lineage-local evaluators

## Forbidden Move
This branch is not allowed to smuggle its intelligence back into Hyena, side memories, or classifier heads and still claim that Circleworld is doing the work.

## Central Evaluator
- major-arc mass vs minor-arc residue
- promotability gain
- packet count by depth
- ontology-survival style local structural laws

## Current Evidence
The branch is now scaffolded and runnable:
- `no_promotion`: `major_gain = 0.0`
- `passive_packets`: `major_gain = -0.00029`
- `active_packets`: `major_gain = -0.00124`

## Success Signature
- promoted packets improve major-arc mass or residue profile
- recursive depth increases reusable structure rather than merely multiplying objects
- local law beats passive summary

## Failure Signature
- promotions happen but do not improve structure
- active law worsens arc mass
- recursion degenerates into decorative object spawning

## Constitutional Verdict
Untested in the strong sense, but now operational as a real branch with honest failure. It is a scaffolded research program, not yet a validated replacement.
